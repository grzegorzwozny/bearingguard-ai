## Purpose

This directory will hold the visual material for documentation and the Hackster.io project page:
a cover image, exported diagrams, photographs of the rig and hardware, and references to videos.

## Rules

- **Only original material** created by the project, or material under a licence that permits
  redistribution with attribution recorded in the directory README. No manufacturer product photos,
  copied diagrams, or stock images without a verified licence.
- **Keep files small.** Images are resized for web use (preliminary limit: a few hundred kilobytes);
  the full-resolution originals stay outside the repository.
- **No video files in git.** Videos are hosted externally and linked; `.gitignore` excludes common
  video formats.
- **No people without consent** and no personal data in photos (name tags, screens with private data).
- **Honest captions.** A caption must state what the image shows and its status (render, mock-up,
  photo of the real rig).
